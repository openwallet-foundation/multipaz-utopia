The Utopia universe

Welcome to Utopia.

A small, entirely invented state where the paperwork actually works. The Registry keeps the records, the DMV and the Bank issue digital credentials to your wallet, and the Marketplace and UPay ask for exactly as much as they need — and not a field more.

Get a driver license Browse the marketplace

Utopia is a demonstration environment for the Multipaz digital-credential stack. Nothing here is real: no real people, no real money, no real groceries. Credentials issued here are test artifacts and are accepted nowhere outside this playground.

Offices of the state

Two issuers, one system of record, and two places to spend what you hold.

R
Registry
The system of record. Every fictional resident and the data the state holds on them — the source every credential is built from.
System of record
D
Utopia DMV
Issues the mobile driving licence to your wallet. Utopia stopped printing plastic; you decide which parts anyone gets to see.
OpenID4VCI · ISO 18013-5 mDL
B
Bank of Utopia
Issues the payment card credential your wallet signs with. No card number is ever typed into a merchant's form.
OpenID4VCI · Payment credential
M
Marketplace
Groceries and everyday essentials. Age-restricted items ask for a digital ID and learn that you are over 18 — never your date of birth.
OpenID4VP · Digital Credentials API
U
UPay
Send money between accounts. Your wallet signs the payment authorization and that is the whole ceremony.
OpenID4VP · ISO 18013-7 Annex C

The Registry has a second front-end: the Registry console, a Kotlin/JS build of the same records service.
